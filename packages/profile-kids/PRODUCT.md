# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Children use a small world-building activity with a parent or guardian able to
inspect the privacy boundary. The primary job is to make and play a tiny scene
without learning the adult editor or entering personal information.

## Product Purpose

Provide one understandable creative loop—pick, add, play—while making safety a
property of the build. Success means a child can complete that loop independently
and no action reaches identity, commerce, catalogs, models, telemetry, persistence,
or an external data plane.

## Brand Personality

Clear, kind, and quietly playful. The surface should feel encouraging without
talking down to the child or overstimulating them.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble the dense SceneAxi desktop editor, a storefront, a social feed, a
chat assistant, an attention-reward loop, or a toy covered in competing decoration.

## Design Principles

- Make the next safe action obvious.
- Prefer a few concrete choices over open-ended input.
- Let play reward what the child made, not engagement metrics.
- Explain a refusal in plain language without naming unavailable product planes.
- Keep grown-up assurance nearby but out of the child's main flow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA text contrast, use large touch targets and visible keyboard focus,
announce state changes, preserve meaning without color, respect reduced motion,
and collect no age or other personal attribute in this first release.
